# Inzonex Research

Open industrial-energy datasets, engineering methodologies and heat-loss tools published by Inzonex.

## Research areas

- Industrial heat loss and surface temperature
- Removable modular thermal insulation
- Steam and condensate systems
- Power-plant and industrial-facility data
- Carbon accounting and industrial decarbonisation
